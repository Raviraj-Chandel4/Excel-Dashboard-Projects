# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand business performance, sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, and product profitability.

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the highest-performing states.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Identify the top 10 products by sales.
- Analyze popular payment methods.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Compare profit across product categories.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** ₹4,93,84,840.00
- **Total Profit:** ₹73,77,771.09
- **Total Orders:** 15,000
- **Average Discount:** 0.15 (15%)
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 45,191

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

- **Sales by State:** Compares sales performance across different states.
- **Sales by Product Category:** Shows sales distribution across product categories.
- **Popular Payment Method:** Visualizes the distribution of payment methods, including Net Banking, Debit Card, UPI, Credit Card, and Cash on Delivery.
- **Top 10 Products by Sales:** Highlights the products generating the highest sales.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Tracks sales performance from January to December.
- **Profit by Product Category:** Compares profitability across different product categories.
