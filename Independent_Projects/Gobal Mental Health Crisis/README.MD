# 🧠 Global Mental Health Crisis Index 2025 Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Global Mental Health Crisis Dashboard** designed to analyze mental health challenges across countries and regions worldwide.

The dashboard helps researchers, policymakers, and healthcare organizations:
- Monitor global mental health trends
- Identify countries with severe mental health burdens
- Analyze regional disparities
- Understand treatment accessibility gaps
- Compare healthcare spending with affected populations

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- Which countries have the highest Mental Health Crisis Index?
- How is the mental health burden distributed across regions?
- What are the average global depression and anxiety rates?
- Which regions have the largest affected populations?
- How does mental health spending compare to the number of affected people?
- What is the global treatment gap in mental healthcare?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Average Depression Rate:** 4.4%
- **Average Anxiety Rate:** 5.6%
- **Average Mental Health Crisis Index:** 58.3
- **Average Treatment Gap:** 58.9%
- **Average Suicide Rate:** 11.6 per 100K
- **Affected Population:** 121.4M / 1428.6M

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Regional Mental Health Burden

| Region | Burden Share |

| Africa | 24% |

| Southeast Asia | 16% |
| Americas | 15% |

🔎 **Insight:**  
Africa contributes the highest share of the mental health burden among all analyzed regions.

### 3️⃣ Depression & Anxiety Trends

- Average Depression Rate: **4.4%**
- Average Anxiety Rate: **5.6%**

🔎 **Insight:**  
Anxiety disorders show a higher prevalence than depression globally, highlighting the need for expanded mental health support systems.

---

### 4️⃣ Treatment Gap Analysis

- Average Treatment Gap: **58.9%**

🔎 **Insight:**  
More than half of individuals requiring mental healthcare do not receive adequate treatment.

---

### 5️⃣ Suicide Rate Analysis

- Average Suicide Rate: **11.6 per 100K population**

🔎 **Insight:**  
Suicide remains a major public health concern and reflects the broader impact of untreated mental health conditions.

---

### 6️⃣ Mental Health Spending vs Affected Population

The dashboard compares:
- Total affected population
- Average mental health spending per capita

🔎 **Insight:**  
Several regions with large affected populations receive comparatively lower mental health investment, highlighting resource allocation disparities.
