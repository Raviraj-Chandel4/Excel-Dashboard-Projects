# 📱 Smartphone Usage Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **Smartphone Usage Analytics Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard analyzes how smartphone usage affects sleep, stress levels, and work productivity across different demographics.

The purpose of this analysis is to understand behavioral patterns related to smartphone usage and how they influence lifestyle and performance.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions
- What is the average daily screen time?
- How does smartphone usage affect sleep duration?
- Does higher screen time increase stress levels?
- How does smartphone usage impact productivity?
- Which age groups spend more time on social media?
- How do different occupations experience stress and productivity?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Average Screen Time:** 6.51 Hours  
- **Average Sleep Duration:** 6.50 Hours  
- **Average Stress Level:** 5.50 / 10  
- **Average Work Productivity Score:** 5.50 / 10  

These KPIs provide a quick overview of smartphone usage patterns and their potential effects.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Screen Time vs Sleep Duration
- Average screen time and sleep hours are very close (~6.5 hours).
- Increased screen usage may contribute to reduced sleep quality.

🔎 Insight:  
Balanced device usage is important to maintain healthy sleep habits.

---

### 2️⃣ Stress Level by Gender
- **Male:** 5.51  
- **Female:** 5.50  
- **Other:** 5.51  

🔎 Insight:  
Stress levels are nearly similar across genders, indicating smartphone usage impacts all groups similarly.

---

### 3️⃣ Social Media Usage by Age Group
- **Young Adults** show the highest social media usage.
- **Middle-aged users** show moderate usage.
- **Older adults** show the lowest engagement.

🔎 Insight:  
Younger generations rely more heavily on social platforms.

---

### 4️⃣ Device Usage
- **Android:** 51%  
- **iOS:** 49%

🔎 Insight:  
Device usage is almost equally distributed between Android and iOS users.

---

### 5️⃣ Productivity by Occupation
Productivity levels vary across occupations:
- Business Owners
- Freelancers
- Professionals
- Students

🔎 Insight:  
Work productivity varies depending on professional responsibilities and smartphone dependency.

---

### 6️⃣ Stress vs Productivity
- Higher stress levels slightly correlate with lower productivity in some occupations.

🔎 Insight:  
Managing stress can positively influence work efficiency.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Encourage balanced smartphone usage to improve sleep quality.
- Promote digital wellness awareness programs.
- Monitor screen time among younger users.
- Implement productivity strategies for high-screen-time professions.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
The **Smartphone Usage Analytics Dashboard** highlights the relationship between screen time, sleep patterns, stress levels, and productivity.

These insights can help individuals and organizations understand digital habits and improve overall well-being and productivity.
