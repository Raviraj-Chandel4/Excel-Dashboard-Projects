# 📊 Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An **Excel dashboard** created to analyze retail sales, profit, customer segments, regions, products, and monthly sales trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses retail sales data to create a visual and interactive dashboard in Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides an overview of business performance and helps identify sales and profitability patterns across different regions, products, customer segments, and months.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Sales:** 14,915,596.9
- **Total Profit:** 1,521,768.9
- **Total Orders:** 8,399
- **Average Discount Rate:** 5%
- **Total Shipping Cost:** 107,854.5

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- **Sales by Region** – Comparison of sales performance across different regions.
- **Sales by Product** – Shows the contribution of different product categories.
- **Monthly Sales Trend** – Tracks sales performance from January to December.
- **Profit by Customer Segment** – Compares profit generated by different customer segments.
- **Sales by Product Category** – Provides a detailed view of sales across product categories and sub-categories.
- **KPI Cards** – Displays important business metrics such as sales, profit, orders, discount rate, and shipping cost.

## 🔍 Key Analysis

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- What is the total sales and profit?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which products contribute the most to sales?
- Which customer segment generates the highest profit?
- How do sales change throughout the year?
- Which product categories have higher sales?
- What are the overall shipping costs and discount rates?
