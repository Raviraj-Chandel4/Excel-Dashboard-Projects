# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard – Monthly Report

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** designed to monitor patient flow, wait time, satisfaction levels, and department referrals.

The objective of this dashboard is to help hospital management:
- Monitor emergency room performance
- Track patient satisfaction
- Analyze patient demographics
- Improve operational efficiency

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- How many patients visited the emergency room?
- What is the average wait time?
- What is the patient satisfaction score?
- Which age group visits the most?
- What percentage of patients are attended on time?
- Which departments receive the most referrals?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Patients:** 513  
- **Average Wait Time:** 36.32 minutes  
- **Patient Satisfaction Score:** 4.96 / 5  

These KPIs help evaluate the overall performance of the emergency department.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Patient Attendance Status
- **62% Delayed**
- **38% On-Time**

🔎 Insight:  
More than half of the patients experience delays. There is scope to optimize staffing and workflow.

---

### 2️⃣ Gender-wise Analysis
- **Male:** 53%  
- **Female:** 47%

🔎 Insight:  
Patient distribution is almost balanced across gender.

---

### 3️⃣ Age Group Analysis
Highest patient visits:
- **0–9 years:** 76 patients  
- **10–19 years:** 69 patients  
- **60–69 years:** 67 patients  

Lowest visits:
- **70–79 years:** 54 patients  

🔎 Insight:  
Young children and elderly patients form a significant portion of ER visits.

---

### 4️⃣ Department Referrals
Top referrals:
- **None (Direct Treatment): 299**
- **General Practice: 103**
- **Orthopedics: 65**

Low referral departments:
- Gastroenterology  
- Renal  
- Neurology  

🔎 Insight:  
Most patients are treated without referral, while General Practice and Orthopedics handle major cases.

---

## 📅 Time-Based Monitoring
The dashboard allows filtering by:
- Month (Jan–Dec)
- Year (2023 & 2024)

This helps track performance trends over time.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Reduce delay percentage by optimizing shift allocation.
- Increase staffing during peak months.
- Strengthen General Practice and Orthopedics capacity.
- Maintain high satisfaction score (4.96) through continuous monitoring.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This dashboard provides a complete overview of emergency room operations by combining:
- Operational metrics
- Demographic insights
- Performance indicators
- Department analysis

It can support data-driven decisions to improve hospital efficiency and patient experience.
