# 🚗 Road Accident Dashboard – Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **Road Accident Analysis Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel to analyze accident trends, casualties, and vehicle involvement.

The goal of the dashboard is to help authorities and analysts understand:
- Accident severity levels
- Vehicle types involved in accidents
- Road conditions affecting accidents
- Location-based accident distribution
- Time trends in accidents and casualties

This analysis can support **road safety planning and accident prevention strategies**.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- How many total casualties occurred?
- What percentage of accidents are fatal, serious, or minor?
- Which vehicle types are involved in most accidents?
- Which road types experience the most accidents?
- How do accidents vary across urban and rural areas?
- What are the monthly trends in accidents?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Casualties**
- **Total Accidents**
- **Fatal Casualties**
- **Serious Casualties**
- **Slight Casualties**

These KPIs help measure the overall severity and scale of road accidents.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Accident Severity
Accidents are categorized into:
- Fatal
- Serious
- Slight

🔎 Insight:  
Most accidents fall under **slight severity**, while fatal accidents represent the smallest but most critical category.

---

### 2️⃣ Vehicle Type Involvement
Major vehicles involved:
- Cars
- Motorcycles
- Vans
- Buses
- Trucks

🔎 Insight:  
Cars and motorcycles contribute the largest share of accidents.

---

### 3️⃣ Road Type Analysis
Road types include:
- Single Carriageway
- Dual Carriageway
- Roundabout
- One Way Streets

🔎 Insight:  
Single carriageway roads show the highest accident occurrence.

---

### 4️⃣ Area Analysis
- Urban Areas
- Rural Areas

🔎 Insight:  
Urban areas typically show higher accident counts due to traffic density.

---

### 5️⃣ Monthly Accident Trends
The dashboard displays accident trends across months to identify **seasonal patterns**.

🔎 Insight:  
Certain months show spikes due to weather conditions, traffic, or holidays.

---

## 💡Recommendations
- Improve safety measures on **single carriageway roads**.
- Increase awareness for **motorcycle safety**.
- Implement stricter traffic monitoring in **urban areas**.
- Use monthly trends to plan **seasonal safety campaigns**.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of road accident patterns by combining multiple dimensions such as severity, vehicle type, road conditions, and location.

These insights can help authorities and planners **develop data-driven strategies to reduce accidents and improve road safety**.
