# 📊 Sales By Orders – Dashboard Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Sales By Orders Dashboard** that analyzes business performance across multiple dimensions such as product categories, customer behavior, geography, and time.

The dashboard helps in understanding:
- Which product categories drive the most revenue  
- How profit changes over time  
- Which states contribute the most sales  
- Who are the most profitable customers  
- Monthly and yearly sales trends  

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The main objectives of this project are:
- To analyze sales distribution across categories and sub-categories  
- To track profit trends over multiple years  
- To compare customer contribution to profit  
- To identify geographical performance  
- To understand seasonal (monthly) sales behavior  

---

## 📊 Key Insights from Dashboard

### 1. Sales by Category
Top-performing sub-categories:
- **Phones** – Highest sales  
- **Chairs**  
- **Storage**  
- **Tables**  
- **Binders**

Lowest-performing:
- Fasteners  
- Labels  
- Envelopes  

**Insight:**  
Technology-related and office furniture products dominate total revenue.

---

### 2. Profit Gained Over Time (2014–2017)
- **Technology** shows the fastest growth in profit.  
- **Office Supplies** grows steadily.  
- **Furniture** remains comparatively low and inconsistent.

**Insight:**  
Technology is the most profitable long-term category.

---

### 3. Sales by State
- **California** generates the highest sales.  
- Followed by New York and Texas.  
- Several states contribute minimal revenue.

**Insight:**  
Sales are highly concentrated in a few major states.

---

### 4. Customer Count (Year-wise)
Customer growth trend:
- 2014: High  
- 2015: Slight drop  
- 2016: Increase  
- 2017: Highest

**Insight:**  
The business is successfully expanding its customer base.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend
- Lowest sales in **February**

Sales show strong **year-end seasonality**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing on **Technology products**.  
- Improve performance in low-revenue states.  
- Retain top customers with loyalty programs.  
- Prepare inventory for **Q4 peak season**.  
- Investigate why Furniture profit is low.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This dashboard provides a clear overview of business performance and highlights that:
- Technology drives profitability  
- Sales are geographically concentrated  
- A few customers generate major profit  
- End-of-year months are the most critical  

The insights can directly support **strategic business decisions**.
