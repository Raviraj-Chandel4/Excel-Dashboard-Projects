# 🛍 Krishna Store Annual Sales Report – 2022

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the **annual sales performance of Krishna Store for the year 2022** using an interactive dashboard.  
The objective is to understand customer behavior, sales trends, channel performance, and regional contribution in order to improve business decisions.

The dashboard provides insights on:
- Sales comparison between men and women  
- Orders by age group and gender  
- Channel-wise order contribution  
- State-wise sales performance  
- Monthly sales and order trends  
- Order status distribution  

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- Which month has the highest sales and orders?  
- Who purchases more: men or women?  
- Which age group contributes the most?  
- Which sales channel performs best?  
- Which states generate maximum revenue?  
- What is the overall order success rate?  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Sales by Gender
- **Women:** 64%  
- **Men:** 36%

**Insight:**  
Women customers contribute **nearly two-thirds of total sales**.

---

### 2. Orders by Age Group & Gender
Highest contributing group:
- **Adult (30–49 years)** – ~50% of total orders  
- Seniors and Teenagers contribute significantly less.

**Insight:**  
The core customer segment is **adult women aged 30–49 years**.

---

### 3. Sales Channels Performance
Top channels:
- **Amazon – 35%**  
- **Myntra – 23%**  
- **Flipkart – 22%**

Other channels (Ajio, Meesho, Nalli, Others) contribute less.

**Insight:**  
Over **80% of total sales come from Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart**.

---

### 4. Top 5 States by Sales
| State | Sales |
|------|-------|
| Maharashtra | 2.99M |
| Karnataka | 2.65M |
| Uttar Pradesh | 2.10M |
| Telangana | 1.71M |
| Tamil Nadu | 1.68M |

**Insight:**  
These 5 states contribute the majority of revenue, with **Maharashtra leading**.

---

### 5. Order Status
- **Delivered:** 92%  
- Cancelled: 3%  
- Returned: 3%  
- Refunded: 2%

**Insight:**  
The store has an **excellent delivery success rate**.

---

### 6. Monthly Sales & Orders Trend
- Highest sales observed in **March and August**.  
- Gradual decline towards the end of the year.  
- Orders and revenue follow a similar pattern.

**Insight:**  
Sales show **seasonal peaks**, useful for inventory planning.

---

## 🎯 Final Business Recommendation
To improve Krishna Store’s sales:

> **Target women customers aged 30–49 years** living in  
> **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**  
> by promoting ads, discounts, and offers on  
> **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This analysis reveals that:
- Women are the primary buyers  
- Adult age group drives maximum revenue  
- Online marketplaces dominate sales  
- A few states contribute most revenue  
- Order fulfillment performance is strong  

These insights can help optimize:
- Marketing strategy  
- Inventory management  
- Regional campaigns  
- Customer retention programs
