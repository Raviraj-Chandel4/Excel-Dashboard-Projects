# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data from a coffee shop chain to uncover trends in customer behavior, product performance, and store-level sales. The goal of this analysis is to provide actionable business insights that can help improve revenue, optimize operations, and enhance customer experience.

The dataset includes information about:
- Transaction time and date  
- Store location  
- Product category and type  
- Quantity sold and revenue  

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The main objectives of this project are:
- To understand when customers are most active  
- To identify top-performing products and categories  
- To compare performance across store locations  
- To analyze customer spending behavior  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Sales by Day and Hour
- **Friday** is the highest revenue-generating day.  
- **Saturday** has the lowest sales.  
- Sales peak during **morning hours (8 AM – 10 AM)**.  
- After **6 PM**, sales drop significantly.

**Insight:**  
The business is driven mainly by **morning customers**, such as office workers and students.

---

### 2. Peak Times & Monthly Revenue
- The busiest time is **8–10 AM**.  
- Monthly revenue shows steady growth:
  - Lowest in **February**
  - Highest in **June**

**Insight:**  
Customer traffic increases over time, indicating business growth and possible seasonal demand.

---

### 3. Store Location Performance
| Location          | Performance |
|------------------|-------------|
| Hell’s Kitchen   | Highest sales |
| Astoria          | Very close |
| Lower Manhattan  | Slightly lower |

**Insight:**  
All stores perform **almost equally**, showing balanced operations.

---

### 4. Customer Spending Behavior
- **Average bill per person:** $4.69  
- **Average orders per person:** 1.44  

**Insight:**  
Most customers make **small, quick purchases**.

---

### 5. Best-Selling Products
Top products by revenue:
1. Barista Espresso  
2. Brewed Chai Tea  
3. Hot Chocolate  
4. Gourmet Brewed Coffee  

**Insight:**  
Hot beverages dominate sales, with **espresso as the top product**.

---

### 6. Sales by Product Category
- Coffee: **39%**  
- Tea: **28%**  
- Others: 33%

**Insight:**  
**Coffee and Tea together generate 67% of total revenue**, making beverages the core business.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Increase staff during **morning peak hours (8–10 AM)**.  
- Offer discounts in **evening hours** to boost low sales.  
- Promote **coffee and tea products** aggressively.  
- Bundle snacks with drinks to increase **average order value**.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This analysis shows that the coffee shop’s success is highly dependent on **morning demand and beverage sales**. By focusing on peak hours, top products, and customer behavior, the business can further improve profitability and customer satisfaction.
