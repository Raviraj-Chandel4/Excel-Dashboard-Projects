# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, and organizational trends.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management:
- Monitor employee turnover
- Identify high-risk attrition groups
- Analyze workforce distribution
- Improve retention strategies

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments face the highest attrition?
- Which age group is most likely to leave?
- Does education level impact attrition?
- Which job roles are most affected?
- How does marital status influence attrition?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Employees:** 1470  
- **Active Employees:** 1233  
- **Attrition Count:** 237  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16%  
- **Average Age:** 36.9 years  
- **Job Satisfaction Rating:** 2.6 / 5  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Gender Distribution
- **Male:** 60% (882 employees)  
- **Female:** 40% (588 employees)

🔎 Insight:  
Workforce is male-dominated, but attrition needs to be monitored across both groups.

---

### 2️⃣ Department-wise Attrition
- **R&D:** 56%  
- **Sales:** 39%  
- **HR:** 5%

🔎 Insight:  
R&D and Sales departments experience the highest attrition impact.

---

### 3️⃣ Age Group Analysis
Highest attrition:
- **25–34 years:** 112 employees  
- **35–44 years:** 51 employees  

Lowest attrition:
- **Over 55 years:** 11 employees  

🔎 Insight:  
Young professionals (25–34) are most likely to leave.

---

### 4️⃣ Education-wise Attrition
- Bachelor’s Degree: 99  
- Master’s Degree: 58  
- Associate Degree: 44  
- High School: 31  
- Doctoral Degree: 5  

🔎 Insight:  
Employees with Bachelor’s degrees show the highest attrition.

---

### 5️⃣ Job Role Impact
Highest attrition roles:
- Laboratory Technician  
- Sales Executive  
- Research Scientist  

🔎 Insight:  
Operational and sales-related roles are most affected.

---

### 6️⃣ Marital Status Analysis
- Married: 673  
- Single: 470  
- Divorced: 327  

🔎 Insight:  
Married employees represent the largest workforce segment.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Improve engagement strategies for employees aged 25–34.
- Focus retention programs on R&D and Sales departments.
- Review compensation and growth opportunities in high-attrition roles.
- Improve job satisfaction (currently 2.6/5).

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
This HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of workforce trends and attrition patterns.  

The analysis highlights that attrition is mainly concentrated among:
- Young professionals  
- R&D and Sales departments  
- Certain operational job roles  

These insights can help HR teams design targeted retention strategies.
